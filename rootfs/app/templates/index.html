Light Placement Guide

Drag lights to their physical locations on the map. Signal strength indicators will help optimize ESP32 controller placement.

🔗 Adding lights

  1. Pair the lights with the BRMesh app on your phone as usual.
  2. In the app, export your configuration (it creates a BRMESH_BACKUP… file).
  3. Click Import from BRMesh App and choose that file from your phone or computer. The mesh key, every light's ID, name and type are read from it.
  4. Install the firmware on your ESP32 controller(s) from the Controllers tab.
Pairing reset lights directly from the ESP32 is not supported: the app's pairing reply comes from its native library, which has not been reverse engineered. Lights paired in the app work right away once the export is imported.
