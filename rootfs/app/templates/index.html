🔗 Pair new lamps

  1. Reset the lamps you want to add, the same way as before pairing them with the BRMesh app (for most lamps: switch the power off and on 5 times, about 1 second each, until it blinks). Lamps already paired with the app must be reset first.
  2. Pick the ESP32 controller closest to them and press Start pairing. The controller looks for reset lamps for 2 minutes and gives each one a free light ID and your mesh key.
  3. Paired lamps are added to the Lights tab. Install the firmware on your controllers afterwards (Controllers tab) so the new lights show up in Home Assistant.
Pairing needs controller firmware 2.1.0 or newer. Lamps paired here do not appear in the BRMesh app, and the app does not know which light IDs the add-on gave out. If you also pair lamps with the app, import its export first so both use the same mesh key and the add-on knows the IDs the app took. Lamps that answer with the app's newer "extended" protocol are listed but cannot be paired yet.
