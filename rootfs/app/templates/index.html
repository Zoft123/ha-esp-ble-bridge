Pair new lamps

  1. Reset the lamps you want to add, as you would before pairing them with the BRMesh app (for most lamps: switch the power off and on 5 times, about a second each, until it blinks). Lamps that are paired with the app have to be reset first.
  2. Choose the controller closest to the lamps and start pairing. For two minutes it looks for reset lamps and gives each a free light ID and your mesh key.
  3. Paired lamps appear under Lights. Install the firmware on your controllers afterwards so Home Assistant gets the new light entities.

Needs controller firmware 2.1.0 or newer. Lamps paired here do not show up in the BRMesh app, and the app does not know which IDs were handed out here; if you also pair with the app, import its export first. Lamps that answer with the app's extended protocol are listed but cannot be paired yet.
